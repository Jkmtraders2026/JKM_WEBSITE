This notice explains how information submitted through this website is used.

Information you provide

Our enquiry form collects your name, contact details, company where provided, sourcing requirements and any reference files you choose to upload. Do not submit passwords, payment-card information or identity documents through this form.

Why we use it

We use your information to review your request, contact you, prepare proposals and coordinate agreed services. Relevant details may be shared with suppliers or logistics partners when needed for the work you request.

Storage and access

Submitted requests are stored in our Supabase-backed system with access restricted to authorised staff. Files are kept in private storage. Staff should retain information only for the enquiry, service and applicable record-keeping needs.

Your choices

Contact us on +255 699 830 601 to request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry information. We may need to verify your identity. Some records may need to be retained for contractual or legal reasons.

External services

WhatsApp and any social platforms you visit operate under their own privacy policies. Where enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile helps protect our form against automated submissions. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies by default.

Contact

For questions about this notice, contact JKM using the phone numbers or WhatsApp link on our contact page.
