COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Can I send a photo instead of a product name?

Yes. Include clear photos, any model numbers, approximate quantities and your destination so we can understand your request.

Are shipping and customs charges included?

Only where explicitly included in your quotation. We clarify which charges are included, estimated or payable separately.

How long does a sourcing request take?

It depends on the product, supplier response, specifications and destination. We discuss the expected next steps after reviewing your brief.

Can you work with a supplier I have already found?

Yes. Tell us whether you need document checks, inspection coordination, purchasing support or logistics coordination.
